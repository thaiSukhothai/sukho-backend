Questions

Buying in Dubai, answered.

The questions we hear most from first-time buyers and overseas investors.

Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign nationals can buy freehold property in Dubai's designated freehold areas, which include most major communities such as Downtown, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah and Dubai Hills Estate. You do not need to be a UAE resident to buy.

How much do I need to invest for a Golden Visa?

The 10-year Golden Visa requires property worth at least AED 2 million. This can be one property or several combined, and off-plan and mortgaged properties can qualify under current rules. For the 2-year investor visa, a sole owner of any registered property can now apply. We will confirm eligibility for your specific property before you buy.

Should I buy off-plan or ready?

Off-plan usually offers lower launch prices and staged payment plans, with a wait until handover. Ready property lets you move in or earn rent straight away, and you can see exactly what you are buying. We will walk you through both based on your goals and timeline.

Can I buy from outside the UAE?

Yes. Many of our clients buy from overseas. We run viewings by live video, share documents digitally, and the transfer can be completed through a power of attorney if you cannot travel.

Do you help businesses find commercial space?

Yes. We help companies buy or lease offices, retail units and warehouses, and we check that the space suits your trade licence and free-zone or mainland setup.
